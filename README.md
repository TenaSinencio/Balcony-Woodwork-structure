#  AI-Assisted Balcony Furniture Design & Manual Fabrication (Built at Age 13)

A custom woodworking project executed at age 13. Designed to fully utilize unused balcony space for urban gardening by transforming raw timber into a multi-tiered structural plant shelf. 

The project bridges early-stage AI generative design, architectural planimetry, material sourcing, manual joinery, and creative stop-motion documentation.

---

##  1. The Problem & Spatial Opportunity
My home balcony had excellent sunlight exposure throughout the day, making it an ideal location for growing plants. However, placing pots directly on the floor would create clutter and waste the potential of the vertical space. To prevent the balcony from remaining an underutilized area, I decided to engineer a custom vertical shelving system tailored to the exact space constraints.

##  3. Precision Planimetry & Dimensional Blueprint
Using the actual physical measurements of the balcony wall, I worked alongside AI tools to draft a precise dimensional blueprint. The structure was constrained to fit the available wall footprint while ensuring enough clearance between shelves for future plant growth.

* **Final Dimensions:** $140\text{ cm}$ (Height) $\times$ $120\text{ cm}$ (Width) $\times$ $28\text{ cm}$ (Depth)
* **Shelf Clearance:** $32\text{ cm}$ vertical gap between levels

##  4. Material Sourcing & Hardware Log
With the technical blueprint finalized, I calculated the bill of materials (BOM) and acquired the necessary raw timber and assembly hardware:
* Solid pine boards (pre-cut to spec dimensions)
* Heavy-duty wood screws 
* Sanding paper range ($80 \rightarrow 120 \rightarrow 220$ grit)
* Protective wood stain/varnish & application brushes
* Basic hand tools: Hammer, square ruler, and drill bits
